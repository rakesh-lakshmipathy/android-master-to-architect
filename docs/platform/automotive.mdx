# Android Auto and Android Automotive OS

**Android Auto** projects a driver-optimized experience powered by the connected phone. **Android Automotive OS (AAOS)** runs in the vehicle and installs the app on the car. A vehicle may support both.

| Concern | Android Auto | AAOS |
|---|---|---|
| Runtime/data | Phone app/services | Vehicle Android device |
| Connectivity | Phone and projection link | Vehicle-assigned networks, possibly no phone |
| Identity | Commonly phone account | Multiple vehicle users/guest/valet require explicit design |
| Hardware | Phone plus car host | OEM vehicle hardware and displays |

Use supported app categories and Car App Library/media templates rather than recreating a phone UI. Templates constrain interaction to reduce driver distraction and give OEM-consistent behavior.

## Restrictions are context, not gear inference

UX restrictions can vary by manufacturer, country, and display: a driver-facing screen may be restricted while a passenger display is not. Query platform restrictions. Do not infer them from “gear != PARK” or try to bypass them.

Design glanceable hierarchy, short flows, large targets, voice/audio continuity, safe errors, and parked-only setup. Preserve playback/navigation intent across phone disconnect, vehicle shutdown, network switching, account change, and process death.

## Architecture boundary

```kotlin
interface DrivingContext {
    val interactionMode: StateFlow<InteractionMode>
}

enum class InteractionMode { PARKED, RESTRICTED, PASSENGER_ALLOWED }
```

Domain/media state should not depend on Activity or car templates. Platform adapters translate supported actions. Backend authorization remains independent of vehicle UI.

## Practical mistakes

- ❌ Mark ordinary AAOS activities distraction-optimized.
- ✅ Use only supported declarations/APIs and current car-quality rules.
- ❌ Assume a persistent personal driver account.
- ✅ handle guest, logout, shared vehicle, and privacy-safe cache clearing.
- ❌ Test only the desktop-sized emulator window.
- ✅ test applicable templates, rotary/touch, audio focus, restriction changes, reconnect, and OEM dimensions.
