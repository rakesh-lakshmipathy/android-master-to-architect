# SLIs, SLOs, alerts, incidents, and postmortems

- SLI: measured indicator of user-relevant service.
- SLO: target for that SLI over a window.
- SLA: external/business agreement with consequences; not every internal target.

## Mobile-aware SLI

```text
Checkout confirmation SLI =
eligible checkout attempts reaching durable confirmed/rejected outcome
within 10 seconds / all eligible checkout attempts
```

Define eligible population, offline handling, app versions, retries, user cancellation, clock, sampling, duplicate events, and missing telemetry. Server-only 200 rate misses UI crashes and local sync failure; client-only telemetry is incomplete/bias-prone. Reconcile signals.

## Error budget

An SLO of 99.9% permits 0.1% bad events in the window. Error-budget burn rate shows how quickly that allowance is consumed. Fast-burn alerts catch acute outage; slow-burn alerts catch chronic degradation without paging on every transient.

## Actionable alert

Every page has:

- user impact/SLO and severity;
- owner/on-call route;
- dashboard and version/cohort dimensions;
- runbook: validate, scope, mitigate, communicate, escalate;
- safe rollback/flag/traffic control;
- expected false-positive/no-data behavior.

Alert on symptoms (checkout failures) before only causes (CPU). Cause alerts help diagnose but may not be user-impacting. Avoid paging on a single crash event or unbounded cardinality.

## Incident flow

```text
detect -> acknowledge -> assign incident commander
 -> assess impact/scope/version/cohort
 -> stabilize (rollback/kill switch/rate limit)
 -> communicate timeline/status
 -> investigate with preserved evidence
 -> recover and verify SLI
 -> postmortem/actions
```

Separate command, operations, and communication roles for significant incidents. Keep a timestamped decision log and explicit hypotheses.

## Mobile mitigation constraints

Users may not update quickly. Maintain backward-compatible server fixes, kill switches/safe config defaults, staged rollout/rollback, session/token revocation, and graceful old-client behavior. A Play rollback does not instantly replace installed bad builds.

## Blameless postmortem

Document impact, detection, timeline, technical and organizational contributors, what went well/poorly, why safeguards failed, and prioritized actions with owner/deadline. Avoid “engineer should be more careful.” Improve tests, defaults, rollout, observability, ownership, and recovery.

<Admonition type="tip" title="Observability closes a control loop">
Measure user outcome, compare to objective, act, and verify recovery. Dashboards without decisions/owners are decoration.
</Admonition>
