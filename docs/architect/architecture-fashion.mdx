# Avoiding architecture by fashion

Patterns and technologies are compressed experience, not universal requirements. “Google uses it,” “everyone uses Clean Architecture,” or “it is modern” is weak evidence for your constraints.

## Fashion-resistance questions

1. What user/business/quality problem exists today?
2. What evidence and baseline show it?
3. Which constraints and team capabilities matter?
4. What is the simplest viable option, including do nothing?
5. What costs/failure modes does the proposed pattern introduce?
6. What uncertainty will a POC reduce?
7. What observable threshold justifies adoption?
8. How do we migrate, operate, reverse and delete old scaffolding?

## Example: MVI everywhere

MVI-style reducers can make complex state transitions explicit and testable. For a static settings screen, mandatory actions/intents/reducers/effects may add concepts without reducing risk. Use the pattern where state/event complexity and debugging benefits exceed ceremony; keep vocabulary consistent in that scope.

Likewise, modularization, repositories/use cases, KMP, Compose, microservices, reactive streams and event sourcing solve specific forces. Require an ADR for broad adoption, one representative vertical slice, negative evidence, team feedback, performance/build measurements and a stop condition.

## Cargo-cult warning signs

- solution appears before requirements;
- only benefits are documented;
- sample app is treated as production proof;
- abstractions are named after frameworks rather than business capabilities;
- dissent asks for evidence and is dismissed as resistance;
- adoption success is percentage migrated, not outcome;
- no owner, fitness function, exit or deletion plan.

## Practical mistakes

- ❌ Ban every new technique as hype.
- ✅ evaluate it against explicit forces and evidence.
- ❌ standardize after a conference demo.
- ✅ trial a hard representative slice and record trade-offs.
- ❌ copy another company's architecture without its scale/team constraints.
- ✅ borrow principles, then re-derive the local design.

## Exercise

Challenge “We must migrate all Android UI to Compose and all business logic to KMP this year.” Produce questions, experiment, metrics, phased decision and stop conditions.
